Audiora: A Free and Simple YouTube to MP3 Converter

A lot of useful audio lives inside videos: lectures, interviews, podcasts and practice tracks. When you only need the sound, Audiora lets you pull it out as an MP3 file you can play anywhere.

What Is Audiora?

Audiora is a free, browser-based tool that converts YouTube videos into MP3 audio. There is no software to install and no account to create. It runs on desktop, tablet and mobile, so you can use it on whatever device you have to hand.

How to Convert a Video to MP3

Copy the link of the YouTube video you want, paste it into the box on the Audiora homepage, choose your audio quality and click convert. Within a few seconds, your MP3 file is ready to download.

Why Choose Audiora?

Many converters are full of pop-ups, redirects and confusing buttons. Audiora keeps things clean, with one input box, a clear download button and no clutter. Every file is saved as a standard MP3, so it works on phones, laptops, car stereos and most media players.

Popular Ways to Use It

People use Audiora to save lectures for offline study, listen to interviews and talks on the move, back up audio from their own uploaded videos, and build playlists of royalty-free music.

Use It Responsibly

Only convert content you own, have permission to download, or that is freely licensed. Respecting creators' rights keeps tools like this available for everyone.
